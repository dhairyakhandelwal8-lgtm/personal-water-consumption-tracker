Conservation tips

Simple actions you can try today
ECO TIPS
🚿
Keep showers shorter

Try reducing shower time and turn off the tap while soaping.

🚰
Turn off running taps

Do not leave water running while brushing or washing dishes.

🧺
Run full loads

When practical, use the washing machine only with a full load.

🔧
Report leaks

A dripping tap or leaking pipe should be repaired promptly.

🌿
Reuse water safely

Where suitable, reuse clean leftover water for plants instead of wasting it.
